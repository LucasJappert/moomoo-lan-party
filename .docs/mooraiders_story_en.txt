🧩 Complete Story of MooRaiders (Initial Version for the First Release)

Prologue
--------
Millennia ago, the Eternal Lands were protected by an ancient creature known as MooMoo, a mystical being who maintained the balance between the forces of chaos and harmony. It was said that its energy was the life source of the world, but it also harbored a forbidden power locked within its core: the Primordial Heart.

Over time, civilizations forgot its purpose and began worshiping it as a sleeping god. But recently, something has disturbed its slumber...

🛡️ Part 1: The Defense of the MooMoo
------------------------------------
Players, heroes from different regions, receive visions or messages from ancient sages who warn them: "The awakening of the MooMoo will bring the end of the world unless its spirit can be contained."

Thus, each player travels to the sanctuary where the MooMoo rests, determined to defend it from hordes of creatures corrupted by the dark energy surrounding the Primordial Heart.

- Waves 1 to 8: Minor enemies, increasingly numerous.
- Waves 2, 4, 6, 8: Boss appearances, sent by mysterious forces that seek to take the MooMoo and release its core.

During these rounds, players believe they are doing the right thing, preventing the world from falling.

🧨 Part 2: The Revelation
-------------------------
After defeating wave 12, when all seems calm, a scene is triggered.

The MooMoo begins to tremble, its energy flickers. A deep, distorted voice resounds:

  "Thank you for protecting me... Now that I've regained my strength... I don't need you anymore."

The MooMoo rises—not as a guardian, but as a colossal being corrupted by its own energy. It has awakened not as an ally, but as the true final boss, a threat born from the imbalance of its repressed powers.

🐉 Part 3: Final Battle – The Awakened MooMoo
---------------------------------------------
The MooMoo now possesses:

- Unique new abilities, based on the game's elements (fire, shadow, electricity, etc.).
- Devastating area attacks.
- Phase transitions: at certain health percentages, MooMoo changes form or summons fragments of its essence.

During the battle, players uncover clues: memory fragments, cryptic phrases, and visions hinting that the MooMoo did not act of its own will, but under the influence of a greater force manipulating it from the shadows.

🌒 Epilogue (after victory or defeat)
------------------------------------
If players win, the MooMoo collapses and its last words are:

  "I have seen beyond the veil... he is coming... soon..."

A dimensional rift begins to open in the sky. The end.

If the players lose, a cutscene shows MooMoo spreading its corruption across the world, and the same phrase is heard... but more distorted.

📦 Possible Future Expansions
-----------------------------
- "The Veil of the Void": a campaign where players explore which entity manipulated the MooMoo.
- "The Heart's Fragments": missions to recover pieces of the Primordial Heart before they are used to open the portal.
- "The Abyss Herald": a new boss that emerged from the rift, more powerful than the MooMoo itself.

🎮 Recommended Narrative Structure for MooRaiders
🎬 1. Short Intro Before Hero Selection
Scene: Dark background or visual of the Eternal Lands (could be a static image with particles or slow fire).

Narrated or subtitled text automatically shown (no interaction yet, prologue style):

"The Eternal Lands tremble. The creature that balances the world... is awakening.
Ancient visions speak of a sealed power.
Only the chosen ones can contain the MooMoo's awakening."

Lasts 10–15 seconds, then transitions to the hero selection scene.

🧙 2. Hero Selection with Integrated Lore
When the player hovers or clicks on a hero, show a brief narrative description:

🧝 Eldrin, the Wanderer
"Guided by fragmented dreams of the Primordial Heart, Eldrin seeks to prevent the world from repeating the mistakes of the past."

This adds more depth to the story with each hero.

🗺️ 3. Map Start (Before Wave 1)
Use a fade-in screen with internal monologue of the selected hero or a mysterious voice:

"I've arrived at the MooMoo's Sanctuary... but I am not alone.
Something in this land whispers death..."

Alternatively, a spiritual guide-type NPC (an Oracle, for example) may appear and give a warning in text before disappearing.

🧠 4. During Gameplay – Minimal and Environmental Narrative
Before each boss wave (waves 2, 4, 6, 8):

Show a message like:

"A dark force approaches... They’re not alone this time."
or
"The Heralds of the Core manifest..."

This provides hints without breaking game flow.

While playing, you can use:

Sound effects of whispers, distant earthquakes, or background rumblings.

Short text at the top of the screen like:

"The MooMoo’s energy becomes unstable..."
"The Primordial Heart beats faster..."

💀 5. Wave 12 – Transition to the Revelation
Once the final wave is won:

⏸ Automatically pause the game and show a short cutscene:

MooMoo begins to glow or tremble.

Background darkens.

Deep, distorted voice:

"Thank you for protecting me... Now that I've regained my strength... I don't need you anymore."

Visual change: MooMoo changes sprite or transforms and appears with a giant health bar.
The final boss fight begins.

🌒 6. Epilogue (After Victory or Defeat)
If victorious: fade out, MooMoo collapses and utters a cryptic phrase:

"I have seen beyond the veil... he is coming... soon..."
(Camera shows the sky breaking apart)

If defeated: fade out, corrupted map, distorted phrase.

📦 Technical Implementation Suggestions
Suggested Scenes:
IntroScene – automatic text (optional, skippable).

HeroSelectionScene – selection with brief lore.

GameScene – map and waves.

CutsceneFinalBoss.tscn – MooMoo transformation/cinematic.

EndingScene – summary + cliffhanger.

Use CanvasLayer + RichTextLabel for floating messages and AnimationPlayer for transitions.
